# Responsible Use

This project is shared in good faith for **peaceful research, education, and experimentation
on your own equipment**. The author asks everyone who uses it to honor that intent.

This is a toy-grade Wi-Fi drone controller. It is **not** designed, tested, or fit for any
safety-critical, autonomous-targeting, or weapons-related purpose, and must not be used as one.

## Intended uses

- Learning how cheap Wi-Fi drones work (networking, protocols, video, control loops).
- Experimenting with and improving **your own** hardware (FPV, autonomy, audio, swarm research).
- Education, demos, and open hobbyist tinkering.

## Not acceptable

Please do **not** use this project to:

- Build, assist, or operate **weapons, munitions, or any device intended to harm** people,
  animals, or property; or for **military / combat / paramilitary** purposes.
- **Surveil, harass, stalk, or endanger** other people, or capture imagery/audio of others
  without consent.
- Interfere with aircraft, restricted airspace, infrastructure, or emergency operations.
- Do anything **illegal**, or violate drone/aviation, privacy, or radio regulations where you are.

Reverse engineering for interoperability and research is legitimate; weaponization and harming
people are not. If your intended use falls on the wrong side of that line, this project is not
for you.
